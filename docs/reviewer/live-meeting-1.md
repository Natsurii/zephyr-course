# Live Meeting 1 — August 28th

**Type:** Live session · **Optional** · **Tag:** none

## What this is

An 86-minute recorded session opening the course. It contains a single lesson,
*Meeting Information* — the invitation, joining details and the recording itself.
Nothing is graded and no tag is produced.

The sessions exist because the written lectures cannot cover everything: they carry
the Q&A and the demonstrations that go wrong live, which are often the most
instructive moments of a course.

## What a reviewer should look at

Nothing, for assessment purposes. Useful context includes:

- The session precedes Module 03, so it belongs alongside the Kconfig work.
- *Meeting Information* is a video, not a task.

## Why it is optional

same period is *LED Blinky* (`l2-task1`), and that is the graded artefact.
